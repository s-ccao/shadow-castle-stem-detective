- The master plate is released by **Junction Bench III**, six stages of fault isolation. Nothing is built and nothing is tuned: the bus is dead, and the player probes test points and names the faulty segment. Open faults read full supply upstream and zero downstream, so two chosen probes box the fault in and the board dims what the readings already exclude; degraded segments still conduct, so from stage 4 nothing reads zero and the culprit is found by comparing drops. A wrong call is answered with the reading that contradicts it. The three benches are three different verbs by design: build (I), track (II), diagnose (III).
- The regulator plate is released by **Junction Bench II**, six stages of continuous regulation. There is no parts rack: the player drags a rheostat wiper, the circuit is always live, and an analogue meter answers in real time. The stage is cleared by **holding** the needle inside the printed green band, not by submitting a value — and from stage 4 the filament heats so the load drifts, turning the task into tracking a moving target by hand. Every reading is evaluated from V = V_src · R_lamp / (R_lamp + R_series).
- The auxiliary junction plate is released by **Junction Bench I**, a six-stage apparatus that teaches series continuity by operation: every link must conduct, and a conductor across the lamp carries current past it. From stage 4 the player can close a complete loop and watch the lamp stay dark while the detour is traced in amber — the same fault that darkened Ashford. The bench is drawn as hardware: hinged blade switches, glass fuses with visible filaments, banded resistors, a bulb with a coiled filament, and charge carriers that only move while the circuit is genuinely live.
- Circuit Room's three junction plates are concealed until earned. Flush with their housings, they offer no plate, prompt, focus box or contact band, so the sequence cannot be brute-forced. Opening the repair blueprint while standing in the room, or drinking a Vision potion, surveys them into place with a staggered charge/ring/settle reveal and files their positions as a Note record. Inspecting a housing while unsurveyed states that a plate is flush with it.
- Circuit Room is completable again. Before this, every point that satisfied the contact test was inside a prop's collision and no switch could be pressed.

The floating star ornaments and divider emblem are gone, the mode stamp no longer overlaps the subtitle, the index column and the painted dossier page share exact top and bottom edges (measured from the artwork's painted region rather than its transparent bounding box), and the index scrollbar hides when the index fits.

Every line is derived from real playthrough state — evidence reuses the accusation table's own names and links each record to the conclusion it supports — and the unlock is read from the existing `normal_ending` / `perfect_ending` flags, so old ending saves are recognised with no schema change.
