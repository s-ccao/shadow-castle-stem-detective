# Project status

**Last updated:** 2026-08-14  
**Phase:** feature-complete prototype entering visual polish, full-flow QA, and portfolio handoff.

## Current playable state

- Main menu, opening cutscene, Wake Room tutorial, Castle Hall, Chemistry, Greenhouse, Circuit, Dining, Library, Final Room, death/retry, and ending flows are implemented.
- The intended opening loop is active: inspect Dr. Lin's desk → collect Wake Room key from the bed → learn the oxygen answer and collect the Chemistry key from the bookshelf → solve the Wake Room door.
- Desk-earned Bag, Key, Note, and Map hubs persist into Castle Hall. The first Hall arrival has a one-time floor-route guide to the Chemistry door, its brass core, and back to the lock.
- Inventory, map, clue journal, alchemy workbench, and final deduction board received visual/interaction redesign passes.
- Every in-world character has been calibrated against actual furniture and door scale at 1024×768. Ordinary standing characters are approximately 91 world pixels tall, the Guardian remains deliberately larger, and fallen Mrs. Lin fits the Final Room floor perspective. Character physics, movement, navigation, and authored collisions remain unchanged.
- The Guardian hunt begins on first Hall arrival. It chases via A* while the player is in Hall, persists as an off-scene Hall patrol while the player investigates rooms, and resumes from a safe persisted position on return.
- A persistent Guardian minimap shows the threat in Hall and from safe rooms, but shares the same power-gated fog as the full Map: before Circuit power it reveals no architecture, and afterward only walked routes become gray.
- Normal Hall entries open with a short Guardian close-up: the detective is held, the Guardian pauses and turns, begins marching toward the player, and the camera returns before lethal contact is enabled. The first powered return deliberately replaces that close-up with the route-memory scan. A live path-based contact estimate updates every 0.12 seconds beside the persistent red minimap signal.
- Hall returns place the Guardian at least 800 world pixels from the player when possible, providing at least 4.32 seconds of measured pre-Circuit Tier-2 reaction. Entering any room switches it to off-scene Hall patrol; all nine Hall return spawn IDs are physics-checked for zero wall overlap and at least three clear exits.
- Room interactions now use visible opaque footprints with a 14px contact band and 10px focus padding. Player-above/behind and player-below/in-front depth reversal is active across Wake, Hall, Chemistry, Greenhouse, Circuit, Dining, Library, and Final Room.
- The one-time blue Hall route uses only horizontal/vertical arrows plus explicit 90-degree corner glyphs. Collision-safe orthogonal smoothing removes diagonal and start-cell U-turn artifacts while reducing marker density.
- Bag recipes and all alchemy ingredients now use a shared item visual catalog. Two formula blueprints and three reagent models fill the former placeholders; Blue Blossom, Moonleaf, and potion hero art remain the approved source assets.
- Note Hub now uses a real focusable close button and its functional native scrollbars; duplicate decorative scrolling was removed, record labels and dossier copy are centered, and long files retain themed brass thumbs.
- Bag Hub categories are contained in a four-column filing rail. Long selected-item descriptions, quantity, requirements, action, and bottom status have separate non-overlapping regions. Blue Blossom and Moonleaf now use GPT-authored herbarium models.
- The staged red-stain dialogue presents Cleaning Powder, Indicator, and Broken Glass as three visible trace samples without opening a Bag notification over the conversation.
- Final Case Board's lever is spoiler-free: it seals an accusation or traces a command without naming the Butler/Mechanic in advance.
- Library now separates learning from assessment. Visible-spectrum knowledge is filed in the tall wavelength case, reflection/absorption knowledge in the violet reflection cabinet, and additive-light knowledge in the reinforced light archive. All three sit in the room interior with visible plaques and a wide standable approach, and each question desk stays locked until its record is filed.
- The three desk games are full five-stage mini-games rather than single questions. Prism Cascade orders 3→7 spectral bands on a dispersion rail; Pigment Bench explains five specimens (leaf, apple, lemon, chalk, soot) with live reflected-beam and perceived-colour feedback; Additive Relay renders cyan, magenta, yellow, white and amber from additively blended lamp discs with per-lamp intensity. Each game shows stage pips, a progress bar, score, a non-solving hint, and per-stage reset; wrong answers never cost progress. Clearing all five stages awards one filter, and all three earned/inserted filters produce pale white archive light.
- The optics laboratory presentation is animated but restrained: a procedural grid/scan/star shader, ambient archive motes and rotating brass instrument reticles provide continuous life; choices pop and spark; wrong answers briefly pulse/shake the modal; stage clears show a seal/ring burst; and a five-stage clear presents a framed, faceted physical filter above a separate result plaque. No effect moves gameplay bodies or blocks input.
- Major light/effect interactions now share one five-phase timing language through `OpticalFxRuntime`: charge → travel → impact → traced beam → sustained state. Prism crystals physically fly from the tray into empty sockets before emitting toward the spectrum; reflected light grows from specimen to a multi-ring sci-fi observer; additive lamps charge physical emitters before their discs ignite; world RGB filters load into the optical stand and send three beams into a rotating archive scanner before white light appears. The same timing now stages Circuit switch power, item rewards, parchment unfurling, Final Board evidence links, and three-feed alchemy extraction.

Bag category counts live in separate badges rather than clipping long names; its detail stack has measured room for the longest title/description/requirement. Bag fills its proportional workbench frame; Note fills a content-safe parchment rectangle; Map art and exploration fog share one coordinate-locked frame so labels and tracking markers cannot drift.
- Bag now exposes exactly four files aligned to the four metal tabs in its authored board: ALL, POTIONS, MATERIALS and PAPERS. Materials merges herbs, reagents, dishes and key fragments; Papers merges formula blueprints and route/repair maps. No second category row exists.

The full Map and Guardian minimap also remain architecturally black. Hall walking is recorded invisibly, so restoring Circuit power immediately converts all walked ground to dim gray memory while unwalked ground stays black across the world, full Map and minimap.

Guardian Tier 2 nearly equals player speed, so Hall-return separation is now 800px: worst-case reaction is 4.32s while +12% per-key escalation remains unchanged.

- The Guardian hunt now has two phases with an in-fiction cause. Phase one: the tracking serum in the player's blood lets the Guardian find the player anywhere, and each recovered room key adds 12% chase speed up to a six-tier cap (145 → 249 px/s). Phase two: examining the Dining Hall evidence yields Mrs. Lin's Purification, Daze and Shroud blueprints; drinking the Purification Potion permanently removes tracking, after which the Guardian stakes out the doorway of the player's next objective room at 44 px/s.
- Post-purification detection is a real sight model: a 300px cone with a ±46° half-angle, a 78px proximity radius, and a grid line-of-sight sample so walls block vision but doorways do not. Sighting restores the full escalated chase speed; losing sight starts a six-second, 96 px/s search of the last known position before the Guardian returns to its stakeout. The Daze Potion stuns it for 7s and the Shroud Potion makes the player unsightable for 12s.
- A bilingual awareness strip under the pursuit bar reports the Guardian's state, escalation tier, effective chase speed and the player's active reagents, so none of the AI behaviour is hidden.
- Archive-facing screens share one lit-room atmosphere. `ArchiveUi.install_screen_atmosphere()` drives a single premultiplied-alpha pass (edge falloff, one flickering lamp, drifting dust, faint grain) and `install_dossier_chrome()` turns each record panel into a physical dossier with brass corner brackets, rivets, a bound left spine and an inner hairline. Intake, interruption, resolution, the accusation board, Bag, Key and the Note dossier all use it; painted backdrops also breathe on a ~30s cycle.

- The survey Map hub deliberately carries **no** light layer. Its field renders unexplored hall geometry in near-black, so any light behind it would lift the walls into a readable building outline; the release gate now asserts both the exclusion and the measured darkness of the blackout field.
- Rooms cast the light their art already paints, through `OpticalFxRuntime.install_lamp()` / `install_arc_emitter()` with runtime-generated textures. Circuit Room lights five wall lamps, the bench lantern and the generator chamber and arcs from its cracked bus; restoring power raises the lamps and calms the arcing. The Library lights all ten painted fixtures and gives each of the three light challenges a beacon in the wavelength it teaches, dim while dormant, brighter when primed, steady once the filter is earned. The Final Room lights its two violet sconces, the sealed door sigil, the vault core and the brass orrery, and the accusation table carries a slow violet core.
- Circuit Room's three plates are drawn hardware rather than flat vector blocks: a cast-iron backplate on four brass bolts, a brass bezel over a slate recess, two porcelain insulator posts, and a hinged blade that swings down into its contact jaw when the charge lands. The blade's open angle is derived from the plate's own headroom, so it can never swing out through its bezel. Open and closed differ by real movement, not by tint.
- The Guardian starts inside the hall's bottom-right at `(1744, 1072)` instead of against the map border. The hall's walls are collision polygons rather than solid grid cells, so the grid reported the border as walkable and the old corner stood the Guardian in the dead strip behind the painted room; `guardian_hunt_flow_test` now asserts the inset.
- The game has audio. A `Master / Music / SFX / UI` bus layout, an original score and ten feedback one-shots, all generated deterministically by `tools/generate_audio_set.mjs` from oscillators and seeded noise — no samples, no third-party audio. The score is three eight-second layers of one D-minor piece (rest / aware / pursued) started on the same frame and never stopped, so intensity is a gain change and the music escalates without a restart or a seam. `GameAudio` exposes only `play()`, `set_music_intensity()`, `duck_music()` and the volume accessors; buses, voice stealing, pitch spread and ducking stay inside it. Buttons sound through `ArchiveUi.apply_button` and pickups through `GameState.item_acquired`, so no screen can be missed. Settings carries separate Music and Effects faders, styled by the new `ArchiveUi.apply_slider()`.
- The pursuit contact estimate withdraws the instant a hub pauses the world. It used to freeze on its last value and show through the hub backdrop, because `_process` cannot run to retract it while paused.
- The alchemy workbench's copy sits on the plate instead of over it. The expected-product heading and the product name/description each rest on a brass-edged specimen plaque rather than floating over reaction smoke, a shelf edge and a test-tube rack, and the redundant `反应阵列` header is gone so the parchment's three readout lines have room. Rack rows reserve their icon's width in the row stylebox, so a name can no longer be drawn beneath its own icon — a defect that had been silently rendering 洞察药剂配方 as the meaningless 寒药剂配方. `ui_design_contract_test` now asserts the clearance per row.
- Bag's header no longer stacks title, case caption and tab heading on one baseline, and its inspection quantity now sits inside the painted plaque instead of straddling its lower edge.
- Castle Hall renders both figures deliberately small against its 1920×1280 floor: the detective at a Hall-only visual scale of 1.45 (65.8px) and the Guardian at 0.80 (80px), keeping the Guardian about 22% taller and heavier. Only visual nodes and foot anchors changed; bodies, collisions, speeds, A*, catch distance and sight geometry are unchanged. The Guardian starts in the far bottom-right corner, resolved to the nearest walkable cell.
- The pursuit contact estimate behaves like a clock: it runs down every frame and re-measures on a 0.12s interval, reporting danger immediately while limiting how fast relief can raise it. Previously it snapped to each sample, so it refreshed without ever appearing to move.
- The master plate is released by **Junction Bench III**, six stages of fault isolation. Nothing is built and nothing is tuned: the bus is dead, and the player probes test points and names the faulty segment. Open faults read full supply upstream and zero downstream, so two chosen probes box the fault in and the board dims what the readings already exclude; degraded segments still conduct, so from stage 4 nothing reads zero and the culprit is found by comparing drops. A wrong call is answered with the reading that contradicts it. The three benches are three different verbs by design: build (I), track (II), diagnose (III).
- The regulator plate is released by **Junction Bench II**, six stages of continuous regulation. There is no parts rack: the player drags a rheostat wiper, the circuit is always live, and an analogue meter answers in real time. The stage is cleared by **holding** the needle inside the printed green band, not by submitting a value — and from stage 4 the filament heats so the load drifts, turning the task into tracking a moving target by hand. Every reading is evaluated from V = V_src · R_lamp / (R_lamp + R_series).
- The auxiliary junction plate is released by **Junction Bench I**, a six-stage apparatus that teaches series continuity by operation: every link must conduct, and a conductor across the lamp carries current past it. From stage 4 the player can close a complete loop and watch the lamp stay dark while the detour is traced in amber — the same fault that darkened Ashford. The bench is drawn as hardware: hinged blade switches, glass fuses with visible filaments, banded resistors, a bulb with a coiled filament, and charge carriers that only move while the circuit is genuinely live.
- Circuit Room's three junction plates are concealed until earned. Flush with their housings, they offer no plate, prompt, focus box or contact band, so the sequence cannot be brute-forced. Opening the repair blueprint while standing in the room, or drinking a Vision potion, surveys them into place with a staggered charge/ring/settle reveal and files their positions as a Note record. Inspecting a housing while unsurveyed states that a plate is flush with it.
- Circuit Room is completable again. Its three switches are painted onto solid props, so each now declares a walkable `contact_rect` band on the floor in front of its housing while the focus highlight and prompt still frame the painted plate. Before this, every point that satisfied the contact test was inside a prop's collision and no switch could be pressed.
- The Note hub was rebuilt around one header band and two aligned columns. The floating star ornaments and divider emblem are gone, the mode stamp no longer overlaps the subtitle, the index column and the painted dossier page share exact top and bottom edges (measured from the artwork's painted region rather than its transparent bounding box), and the index scrollbar hides when the index fits.
- Finishing either ending unlocks a **Case Archive** on the main menu with Evidence, People, Timeline, Science and Verdict sections. Every line is derived from real playthrough state — evidence reuses the accusation table's own names and links each record to the conclusion it supports — and the unlock is read from the existing `normal_ending` / `perfect_ending` flags, so old ending saves are recognised with no schema change.
