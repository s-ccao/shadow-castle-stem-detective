# Concept key art: provenance

The key art for the six upcoming games was generated by the project owner with
ChatGPT (OpenAI image generation) on 2026-10-27, from the prompts in
[`prompts.md`](./prompts.md) with `web/landing/assets/hero-castle.jpg` as the
style reference. The machine-readable record, including the SHA-256 of each
delivered file, is in [`manifest.json`](./manifest.json).

| Game | Site master | Native resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence Rush | `source-native-1672x940.png` | 1672 × 940, an independent generation |
| Prism Lab, Echo Corridor, Time Echo, Alchemy Shift, Rune Defense | `key-art-2048x1152.png` | an 836 × 313 panel of a 1672 × 941 six-panel sheet, enlarged to 2048 × 1152 by the delivery; not native high resolution |

**Where it is used.** Case cards (800 and 480 wide), the case-file library's
list (320 wide) and each case page's sharing image (1200 × 630). Five images are
enlargements of small panels, so they are not used at stage or hero size: the
case hero and the library stage keep the mechanic vignettes until each game has
a native generation (run its prompt on its own, 16:9, at least 1920 wide).

**Honesty.** Every card that shows concept art carries a visible "Concept art"
label, and `tools/check_site.py` fails if a concept image is not marked
`"label": "concept"`.

**Edits.** Cropped (16:10 for cards, 1200 × 630 for sharing, around the subject)
and downscaled with Chrome's canvas at high-quality smoothing, then encoded as
WebP (q0.86 cards, q0.84 thumbnails) and JPEG (q0.88). No retouching.

**Replacing an image.** `/assets/*` is cached for a year, so a new version gets
new file names (`key-art-v2.*`); update the catalogue entry and this record.
