# Key-art prompts (concept art for the six upcoming cases)

Attach `web/landing/assets/hero-castle.jpg` as the **style reference** for every
prompt. Ask for 16:9 at 2048 × 1152 or larger. Generate 2–4 candidates each and
save them to `~/Desktop/shadow-castle-key-art/<slug>/`.

Shared ending for every prompt (append it):

> Style: high-detail pixel-art illustration matching the reference image, painterly
> lighting, deep violet night palette (#030309 to #191231), cool moonlight (#b9def4)
> falling from the upper left, warm brass (#e8a127) practical light. Composition:
> the focal subject sits in the centre-right; keep the lower-left third dark and calm
> for overlaid text. Exclude: text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, frames, borders,
> watermarks, signatures, modern objects, photorealism, close-up faces.

## evidence-rush — Evidence Rush · 极速侦探 (accent #f45a56 crimson)

ROLE: concept key art for a fast deduction game.
SUBJECT: a candlelit castle study at night; a large cork evidence board covered in
pinned testimony cards, sketches and wax-sealed letters; crimson threads run between
the cards and converge on one card that glows crimson where two statements
contradict; a brass stopwatch hangs on a chain in the foreground, its hand near the
end of the minute.
VIEW: three-quarter view of the board, slight low angle.
LOCKS: crimson #f45a56 is the brightest colour, only on the threads and the glowing card.

## prism-lab — Prism Lab · 光学迷宫 (accent #ee8dda prism magenta)

ROLE: concept key art for an optics puzzle game.
SUBJECT: a castle observatory chamber; a single beam of white moonlight enters through
a high arched window, bounces between rotating brass-framed mirrors on pedestals and
passes through a large crystal prism that fans it into a spectrum; the spectrum lands
on a sealed stone door whose engraved lock glows prism magenta.
VIEW: wide interior, eye level, the beam path readable from left to right.
LOCKS: magenta #ee8dda glow on the prism and the lock; the beam stays white before the prism.

## echo-corridor — Echo Corridor · 回声走廊 (accent #60d8ca sonar teal)

ROLE: concept key art for a stealth game played in the dark.
SUBJECT: a pitch-black stone labyrinth corridor; a detective in silhouette (small, seen
from behind) has just sent out a pulse: teal concentric sound rings expand along the
floor and briefly outline the walls, arches and a junction; far down the corridor a
guard's silhouette turns, a faint teal awareness cone in front of it.
VIEW: looking down the corridor, one-point perspective, the rings mid-expansion.
LOCKS: only the sound rings and the outlined edges are lit; everything else stays black-violet.

## time-echo — Time Echo · 时间残影 (accent #a6a1fd ghost violet)

ROLE: concept key art for a time-loop puzzle game.
SUBJECT: inside the castle clock tower among huge brass gears; the detective stands on
a pressure plate while a translucent violet echo of their past self holds a lever that
keeps a gate open; a dotted trail of ghost footprints links the two; rewinding
particles drift backwards around the echo.
VIEW: side-on cutaway of the tower floor, gears behind, the gate in the right third.
LOCKS: the echo and its trail glow ghost violet #a6a1fd; the present-day detective is warm-lit.

## alchemy-shift — Alchemy Shift · 魔法实验室 (accent #86da73 elixir green)

ROLE: concept key art for a potion-crafting management game.
SUBJECT: an alchemist's tower workshop at night; a long bench of glass flasks, copper
stills and tubing; two coloured liquids pour together in a round flask and turn a
glowing elixir green; order slips are pinned on a board above the bench; bubbles,
sparks and drifting motes rise from the flask.
VIEW: three-quarter view along the bench, the mixing flask as focal point.
LOCKS: elixir green #86da73 is the brightest light; brass and copper catch it.

## rune-defense — Rune Defense · 符文防线 (accent #f68443 rune ember)

ROLE: concept key art for a rune tower-defence strategy game.
SUBJECT: the castle's main gate at night; glowing runes carved into the courtyard
flagstones form a defensive ring before the gate; shadowy monsters advance through the
mist from the left; where they cross the ring, a chain reaction of ember-orange rune
light arcs from stone to stone.
VIEW: elevated three-quarter view of the courtyard, the gate on the right.
LOCKS: rune ember #f68443 is the brightest light; the monsters stay silhouettes.
