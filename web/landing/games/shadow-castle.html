Every room teaches the next step of the case.

Science is not a detached quiz layer. A formula, observation, or measurement becomes evidence, a key, or a survival tool somewhere else in the castle.

Learn by operating the evidence.

Brew field potions by placing real materials, repair electrical systems by tracing the fault, and use optics, biology, and timelines to separate suspicion from proof.

Walk the castle.

Captured in the current build, in the language you are reading: switch languages and the screenshots switch with you.

A prototype documented in public.

The repository records the revisions—not just the result: rejected realistic character art, interaction failures, spatial tests, visual regressions, and the reasoning behind each redesign.

  1. v0.1–0.5 Movement became investigation.

    A maze prototype gained evidence, consequences, STEM gates, and a readable opening.

  2. v0.6–0.14 The castle became a system.

    Animated NPCs, dual endings, spatial contracts, and staged optics linked story to play.

  3. v0.15–0.22 The systems learned to speak clearly.

    Hubs, pursuit, audio, maps, alchemy, and circuit hardware were rebuilt around legibility.
